How to read impact indicators

Numerical impact factors and quartiles are publisher-reported, with a source and metric year or release edition. JIF means Journal Impact Factor. Q1 is shown only where the source explicitly reports it; the category or publisher’s “best quartile” definition is shown in the row details. JCR and SJR quartiles are different systems.

When a current numerical metric was not verified, Est. visibility supplies a qualitative editorial estimate. It is not an estimated JIF or a computed quartile. Indicators describe venues; topical fit still matters.

Regular journals generally accept submissions throughout the year; conference-linked tracks such as PACM HCI follow their own calls. Consult the journal website for the current submission route.
