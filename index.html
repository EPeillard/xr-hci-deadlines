How to read rankings and estimated dates

ICORE 2026 gives conference-level classifications (A*, A, B, C, or non-grade categories). Each verified badge links to its source. It does not rank individual papers, posters or journal tracks.

Est. visibility is an editorial audience-reach estimate, not an official rank or measured citation impact. National, community and industry venues are kept distinct.

Estimated dates are month ranges based on observed editions and the stated recurrence. Single-edition estimates allow an extra month either side; sparse histories use a broad seasonal or 2–5-month lead-time assumption. Open a row to inspect the evidence and confidence. A forecast does not establish that an edition or call has been announced. Organizer-published tentative dates have their own label.

Exact deadlines retain their published timezone and are displayed in Europe/Paris. AoE falls on the following day in France. Calls without a verified cutoff time show a date only. Calendar exports contain only verified dates; forecasts and tentative dates are excluded.
